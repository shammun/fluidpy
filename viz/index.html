Ch. 1 · explainer 2 · §1.3 1.5 How does the fluid learn that a plate moved?

The top plate starts moving: watch momentum diffuse down through the gap, the profile relax to a straight line and the wall stress settle at μU/h, with ν, not μ, setting the clock. Heat (Fourier) and species (Fick) modes show the same diffusion.

Ch. 1 · explainer 3 · §1.8, 1.9 Same two states: what depends on the path?

Take 1 kg of air between the same two states along different routes: heat and work change, internal energy and entropy do not, and friction breaks ds = dq/T.
