## 1. What clicked — the teaching spine (our words, with equation numbers)
1. **C06 Continuum hypothesis (§1.4).** A value "at a point" is the plateau of a box average: the box must hold enough
   molecules for the counting noise N^(−1/2) to vanish (D35) and be small against the flow's own variation. Kn = l/L
   says when the window closes. Pressure is the averaged momentum delivered by molecular impacts (D34).
2. **C12 Newton's law of viscosity (1.3)–(1.4).** Stress is a momentum flux down a velocity gradient, exactly like Fick
   (1.1) and Fourier (1.2). ν = μ/ρ (not μ) sets the diffusion clock h²/ν; air spreads motion faster than water.
3. **C20 Hydrostatic law (1.8).** A still cube balances weight against the top–bottom pressure difference (D05); the
   same face integral of (1.9) is Archimedes' buoyancy (D37). Isotropy (1.6) and Pascal (1.7) are stated inside.
4. **C25 First law (1.10)–(1.11).** Two routes between the same two states: q and w differ, Δe does not.
5. **C35 Gibbs relations (1.18).** Eliminating the path-dependent dq leaves relations among state functions that hold
   for any process (D10); entropy is the heat-like quantity that is not path-dependent.
6. **C36 Speed of sound (1.19).** c² = (∂p/∂ρ)_s measures stiffness of the equation of state; c → ∞ is the
   incompressible limit (proof deferred by the book to Ch. 15).
7. **C40 p = ρRT (1.22).** The molecular law (1.21) divided by volume, with R = R_u/M_w per kilogram (D11; kmol trap).
   Isothermal atmosphere and scale height H = RT/g are stated inside.
8. **C45 Isentropic law (1.25).** ds = 0 in both Gibbs forms, divided and separated, gives p/ρ^γ = const (D14);
   ratios (1.26) and c = √(γRT) (1.27) follow in one line each.
9. **C50 Displaced parcel.** Newton II with buoyancy on a parcel that keeps its own density, linearised to first order
   in ζ, gives ζ″ + N²ζ = 0 (D18; distinct roots → cos/cosh, double root → ζ = const).
10. **C51 Brunt–Väisälä N² (1.29).** The coefficient of that ODE: N² > 0 oscillation at N, < 0 exponential runaway
    (e-folding 1/√−N²), = 0 neutral. Ocean form (1.35) holds as "same sign", not equality.
11. **C54 Adiabatic lapse rate (1.30).** An isentropic parcel in hydrostatic surroundings changes temperature at
    Γ_a = −gαT/C_p (= −g/C_p for a perfect gas, ≈ −9.76 K/km) — derived without perfect-gas relations via a Maxwell
    relation (D19, ★★★, sympy). **Kundu Γ ≡ dT/dz: stable when dT/dz > Γ_a; meteorology Γ ≡ −dT/dz: stable when
    Γ < +9.8 K/km. Negating the number flips the inequality.**
12. **C55 Potential temperature (1.31)–(1.32).** The temperature a parcel would have at the reference pressure (D20);
    stability is dθ/dz > 0 and N² = (g/θ)dθ/dz (D36).
13. **C64 Dimensional homogeneity.** Laws cannot depend on the unit system, so every term carries the same dimensions;
    a dimensionless group keeps its value under SI → cgs → imperial.
14. **C67 Dimensional matrix (1.39).** Dimension vectors as columns; rank r = size of the largest nonzero minor.
15. **C69 Buckingham Π (1.37).** Groups are null-space vectors of the matrix; rank–nullity gives n − r (D28, ★★★,
    sympy re-runs the step-9 construction and checks the basis).

## 4. Mathematics → Python lessons (new pitfalls)
- **A default placed "at" a regime boundary must be computed from the boundary function, never typed rounded.** The
  "neutral" mixed layer defaulted to −9.8e-3 K/m, steeper than Γ_a = −9.7607e-3, so it was labelled unstable
  (review Must-fix 1). Now `MIXED_LAYER_DT_DZ = adiabatic_lapse_rate()`.
- **Prove a test discriminates.** Six parcel tests passed on a copy that divided by the environment density; only an
  exact energy first integral of the unlinearised law (÷ρ_parcel) caught it. Patch a scratch copy, confirm a failure.
- **Statistical tests**: seed every sampler and set tolerances at 5 standard errors of the estimator.
- **pint**: kmol carries a [substance] dimension — `dimension_vector` drops it with a warning (harmless when M_w and R_u
  both appear); °C is an offset unit — kelvin inside functions, `delta_degC` for differences.
- **Exact rationals** (`fractions.Fraction`) for Π exponents, so the JS mirror (a small Fraction class) reproduces every
  number bit for bit and ½ never prints as 0.49999.
- **Temperature scales in old equations of state**: EOS-80 is defined in IPTS-68 (t68 = 1.00024 t90); skipping the
  conversion misses the UNESCO check values by ≥ 1.8e-5 kg/m³ (about twice the table's last printed digit; the
  converted version agrees to ≤ 5e-6).
- **Altitude kind**: USSA-1976 tables are in geopotential altitude; `standard_atmosphere(z, geometric=True)` converts.
  Name the argument's meaning in the docstring and at every call site.
- **Neutral tolerance bands differ between sibling functions** (`lapse_rate_stability` 1e-9 K/m vs
  `classify_stability` 1e-12 s⁻², F3): sweeps comparing them must exclude the band.
- **Argument-order traps** between siblings (`irreversible_process(kind, T1, v1)` vs `path_heat_work_totals(kind, v1,
  T1)`; `molecular_pressure(n, m, velocities)` vs `wall_impact_pressure(velocities, m, n)`): call by keyword.
- **Runaway ODEs** (N² < 0): cap with a `solve_ivp` event (`zeta_max`) and return NaN past it, never inf.
- **Labels mean what they say**: V4 only for genuine conservation laws or state-function invariants; invariance under
  a change of units or convention is V7; an algebraic identity (θρ_θ = p_ref/R) is V1.
- **Book suspected typos** taught as corrected and recorded: Clausius–Duhem with the actual δq/T; (1.35) as "same
  sign"; Ex. 1.1's α is the complement of the contact angle; Step 5 of §1.11 cites (1.39), not (1.32).
- **JS (machinery)**: `UIEvent.view` is a read-only getter — assigning `ev.view` threw in every `onPointer` handler
  and no audit caught it (fixed in b45cbef: `ev.vizView` + `defineProperty`).

## 5. Conventions used (all new in ch01; see `knowledge/notation.md`)
- SI, dimensional code throughout; temperatures in K inside functions. Π groups are the only non-dimensional outputs.
- **z positive upward** (§1.7, §1.10); depth h = −z. ζ = upward parcel displacement from rest height z_o.
- **Lapse rate: Kundu Γ ≡ dT/dz** in all code (`dT_dz` arguments; `adiabatic_lapse_rate()` ≈ −9.76e-3 K/m). The
  meteorology Γ ≡ −dT/dz is always shown alongside (`lapse_rate_convention`, `lapse_rate_stability(convention=
  "meteorology")`); converting negates the number and flips the inequality.
- **p0 ≠ p_ref**: `p0` = pressure at z = 0 (Eq. 1.9, `core.statics`); `p_ref` = `P_REF` = 1.0e5 Pa, the reference of θ
  and ρ_θ. The book takes its reference as the surface pressure p(0); meteorology and our code use exactly 1000 hPa
  (θ at the ground with a sea-level surface is ≈ 0.996 T, about 1 K below T).
- **First law**: q = heat added to, w = work done on the system, per unit mass; reversible work −p dv.
- **Gas constants per kmol** (`R_U` J/(kmol K), `M_W_AIR` kg/kmol); `R_AIR` from CODATA R_u (differs from USSA R* by
  1.7e-5).
- **Laplace jump**: p on the concave side is higher; signed principal radii.
- **Ex. 1.1 α** is measured from the horizontal (α = 90° − θ_c); `alpha_from_contact_angle` converts.
- **Wall stress** `wall_shear_history` returns the signed τ_xy = μ ∂u/∂y at both walls (the stress *on* the moving
  plate is −μ ∂u/∂y).
- **Dimension basis** (M, L, T, Θ), substance dropped; unit systems SI / cgs / imperial in `UNIT_SYSTEMS`.
- **Grid** (`core.diffusion`): values at nodes y_i = y_0 + iΔy, boundary nodes are the walls; FTCS is our choice.
- **Seawater**: S in g/kg passed as practical salinity (≈ 0.5 % DEVIATION); `seawater_density_eos80` takes ITS-90 K.
- No earlier chapter, so no changes to report.
