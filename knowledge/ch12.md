## 1. What clicked — the teaching spine (our words, with equation numbers)
**An average is a sum, a sum commutes with every linear operation, and the one thing it does not commute with — a
product — is the whole subject.** Everything after that is the leftover covariance: what it does to the mean flow (a
stress), to the energy (production), how it is spread over scales (the cascade), how it is guessed (closures), how
stratification kills or feeds it, and how it spreads a tracer.
1. **C01 Ensemble averages.** $\overline{u^m}=\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^{N}u(\mathbf x,t{:}n)^m$ (12.1); the
   time average $\bar u=\frac1{\Delta t}\int_{t-\Delta t/2}^{t+\Delta t/2}u\,dt$ (12.2) replaces it only when the window
   is long against the memory and short against the drift. Rules (12.4)–(12.9) are linearity (D01);
   $\overline{\tilde u\tilde v}=\bar u\bar v+\overline{uv}$. A mean is as good as its number of **independent** samples
   (N members, or Δt/Λ_t memory times).
2. **C02 Autocorrelation.** $R_{11}(\tau)=\overline{u_1(t)u_1(t+\tau)}$ (12.17), even in τ (D03);
   $\Lambda_t=\int_0^\infty r_{11}\,d\tau$ (12.18) is the memory; $\lambda_t^2=-2/[d^2r_{11}/d\tau^2]_{\tau=0}$ (12.19) is
   the foot of the osculating parabola (D04). The bound $\lvert\overline{uv}\rvert\le(\overline{u^2}\,\overline{v^2})^{1/2}$
   (12.16) is on the absolute value (D02; the page prints it one-sided — a trap, not a slip).
3. **C03 Spectrum.** $S_e(\omega)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}R_{11}(\tau)e^{-i\omega\tau}d\tau$ (12.20),
   $R_{11}(\tau)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}S_e(\omega)e^{i\omega\tau}d\omega$ (12.21),
   $\overline{u_1^2}=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}S_e\,d\omega$ (12.22), $S_e(0)=\overline{u_1^2}\Lambda_t/\pi$ (D05): long
   memory ⇔ narrow spectrum; frozen field t ↔ x/U₀ turns it into a wavenumber spectrum.
4. **C04 Reynolds averaging.** $\frac{\partial U_i}{\partial t}+U_j\frac{\partial U_i}{\partial x_j}=
   -g[1-\alpha(\bar T-T_0)]\delta_{i3}+\frac1{\rho_0}\frac{\partial\bar\tau_{ij}}{\partial x_j}$ (12.30) with
   $\bar\tau_{ij}=-P\delta_{ij}+2\mu\bar S_{ij}-\rho_0\overline{u_iu_j}$ (D06, 13 steps). $\overline{uv}<0$ where
   dU/dy > 0 (displaced parcels); no equation for the stress — the closure problem.
5. **C05 Isotropy.** $R_{ij}=\overline{u^2}\{f\,\delta_{ij}+\frac r2f'(\delta_{ij}-r_ir_j/r^2)\}$ (12.41), so
   g = f + (r/2)f′, Λ_g = Λ_f/2, λ_g = λ_f/√2 (D07 ★★★); gradient moments 2 : 4 : −1 give
   $\bar\varepsilon=30\nu\overline{u^2}/\lambda_f^2=15\nu\overline{u^2}/\lambda_g^2$ (12.43) (D08 ★★★). Three-dimensional
   results: a 2-D solenoidal field obeys g = d(rf)/dr.
6. **C06 Energy budgets.** Mean flow (12.46) and turbulence
   $\frac{\partial\bar e}{\partial t}+U_j\frac{\partial\bar e}{\partial x_j}=\frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\big(-\frac1{\rho_0}
   \overline{pu_j}+2\nu\overline{u_iS'_{ij}}-\frac12\overline{u_i^2u_j}\big)-2\nu\overline{S'_{ij}S'_{ij}}-
   \overline{u_iu_j}\frac{\partial U_i}{\partial x_j}+g\alpha\overline{u_3T'}$ (12.47) (D09, D10 ★★★): **production is
   one term with opposite signs in the two budgets**; in the model channel it peaks at 0.245 u_*⁴/ν near y⁺ ≈ 10, where
   viscous and Reynolds stress are equal.
7. **C07 Kolmogorov scales.** $\bar\varepsilon\sim(\Delta U)^3/L$ (12.49); $\eta=(\nu^3/\bar\varepsilon)^{1/4}$,
   $u_K=(\nu\bar\varepsilon)^{1/4}$ (12.50); $\eta/L\sim\mathrm{Re}_L^{-3/4}$ (12.51) (D11): viscosity does not set how
   much is dissipated, only how far down the ladder the eddies must go.
8. **C08 −5/3.** $S_{11}(k_1)=C_1\bar\varepsilon^{2/3}k_1^{-5/3}$ (12.54, slip #1 corrected) from one Π group (D12);
   one-sided C₁ = (18/55)C = 0.491 for C = 1.5, two-sided 0.2455.
9. **C09 Plane jet.** Invariant $J_s=\rho\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}U^2dy$ (12.62) + self-preservation (12.63) ⇒ δ ∝ x,
   $U=C_5(J_s/\rho)^{1/2}x^{-1/2}F(y/x)$ (12.66), V̇ ∝ x^{1/2} (12.68) (D13–D15; D14 ★★★); the same two-line recipe with a
   different invariant gives all seven free shear flows (D16).
10. **C10 Law of the wall.** $\bar\tau=\tau_0(1-2y/h)$ (D17); $U^+\equiv U/u_*=f(y^+)$ (12.80), $u_*^2\equiv\tau_0/\rho$
    (12.81), $U^+=y^+$ (12.82) (D18).
11. **C11 Log law.** Overlap matching $y^+\,df/dy^+=1/\kappa$ (12.87) ⇒ $U^+=\frac1\kappa\ln y^++B$ (12.88) (D19); rough
    wall $U=\frac{u_*}\kappa\ln(y/y_0)$ (12.93) (D20). **A quoted κ belongs to a fit window** (DNS at Re_τ = 5186: 0.400 from
    y⁺ = 30, 0.384 from y⁺ = 350).
12. **C12 Eddy viscosity and mixing length.** $\overline{u_iu_j}=\frac23\bar e\,\delta_{ij}-\nu_T\big(\frac{\partial U_i}
    {\partial x_j}+\frac{\partial U_j}{\partial x_i}\big)$ (12.94); l_T = κy gives the right slope and B = (ln 4κ − 1)/κ =
    −1.23; van Driest damping (A⁺ = 26) moves it to 5.28 (D21): κ turns the line, damping slides it.
13. **C13 k–ε.** $\nu_T=C_\mu\bar e^2/\bar\varepsilon$ (12.104); constants are tied by decay n = 1/(C_ε2 − 1) and the log
    layer κ² = √C_μ(C_ε2 − C_ε1)σ_ε → 0.433 with the standard set (D22, D23).
14. **C14 Richardson numbers.** $\mathrm{Rf}=\frac{-g\alpha\overline{wT'}}{-\overline{uw}\,(dU/dz)}$ (12.107),
    $\mathrm{Ri}=N^2/(dU/dz)^2$ (12.108), $\mathrm{Ri}=(\nu_T/\kappa_T)\,\mathrm{Rf}$ (12.109) (D24). Rf_cr ≈ ¼ is an
    observation; Ri > ¼ of ch11 a theorem.
15. **C15 Monin–Obukhov.** $L_M\equiv-u_*^3/(\kappa\alpha g\overline{wT'})$ (12.110), $\mathrm{Rf}=z/L_M$ (12.111),
    $U=\frac{u_*}\kappa[\ln\frac z{z_0}+5\frac z{L_M}]$ (D25): L_M is a height — below it the wind makes the turbulence.
16. **C16 Taylor dispersion.** $\overline{X_\alpha^2}(t)=2\overline{u_\alpha^2}\,t\int_0^t(1-\tau/t)\,r_\alpha(\tau)\,d\tau$
    (12.119) (D26): ballistic $X_{rms}=u_{rms}t$ (12.121), then a random walk $X_{rms}=u_{rms}\sqrt{2\Lambda_tt}$ (12.123);
    $D_T=\overline{u_\alpha^2}\int_0^tr_\alpha\,d\tau$ (12.127) grows before it saturates at
    $\overline{u_\alpha^2}\Lambda_t$ for $t\gg\Lambda_t$ (12.129, slip #12 corrected) (D27, D28).

## 4. Mathematics → Python lessons (new pitfalls only; each a rule, with the measured evidence)
- **(a) A default must not encode a physical assumption.** `gradient_richardson_thermal` defaulted to `Gamma_a=0.0`, so
  the in-situ gradient of the standard atmosphere (dT/dz = −6.5 K/km, dU/dz = 0.01 s⁻¹, α = 1/288 K⁻¹) gave Ri = −2.213,
  "unstable", instead of +1.110, "stable", for $\mathrm{Ri}=N^2/(dU/dz)^2$ (12.108) with N² = gα(dT/dz − Γ_a). The test
  suite had already named that call a mutant. Rule: **convention-carrying arguments are required keywords**; callers with a
  θ-gradient write `Gamma_a=0.0` on purpose; parity rows pass `ch12.adiabatic_lapse_rate()`, never a typed −0.0098.
- **(b) Fitted constants belong to their window.** `WT.fit_log_law` recovers (κ, B) of an exact log profile to 1e-10, yet
  on Spalding's composite at δ⁺ = 5000 over 30 < y⁺ < 0.15 δ⁺ it returns 0.383, 3.99: the composite reaches the logarithm
  from below (−0.66 at y⁺ = 30, −0.16 at 100, −0.03 at 300). On DNS at Re_τ = 5186: 0.400, 4.92 from y⁺ = 30; 0.3845 from
  3√Re_τ; 0.3837, 4.28 from 350. State the window beside every fitted constant.
- **(c) A key name must carry its sign.** `uv_plus` in the wall-profile, channel and table outputs holds **minus**
  $\overline{uv}$, while `mean_energy_budget` and `tke_budget` take the true correlation; the alias `minus_uv_plus` was
  added and three mutants (R04–R06) pin it.
- **(d) One-sided vs two-sided constants.** `inertial_spectrum_1d(C1=…)` always takes the one-sided C₁ and the default
  `two_sided=True` returns half (1e-14); a two-sided constant goes in as `C1=2*c2`. `spectrum_variance` forgetting the even
  half was mutant M03.
- **(e) An integral scale "to the first zero" is not $\Lambda=\int_0^\infty r\,d\tau$ (12.18) when r has a negative lobe** —
  exactly the transverse g(r): the default gives 0.629 Λ_f where Λ_g = Λ_f/2 (26 % too large). Use `upto="all"`.
- **(f) Estimators have artefacts with a predictable size.** The biased correlation estimate multiplies the true
  correlation by the triangle 1 − r/L, whose kink transforms into a tail $R_{11}(0)/(\pi Lk_1^2)$; the periodic correlation
  `ifft(|fft|²)/n` and a cosine sum over one period reproduce the periodogram to 3e-5. **The first explanation written for
  that tail (taper "leakage") was wrong — test an explanation by removing the alleged cause.**
- **(g) Sampled statistics are estimates.** Every sampled cell fixes its seed; assertions use 5 standard errors (P281);
  measured scatter exponents −0.53 and −0.55 against −½. An Ornstein–Uhlenbeck signal has a cusp in r at τ = 0, so its
  Taylor microscale does not exist — `TS.smooth_signal` for $\lambda_t^2=-2/[d^2r_{11}/d\tau^2]_{0}$ (12.19).
- **(h) Synthetic fields are kinematic.** Random-phase fields have the prescribed spectrum and are solenoidal but have no
  cascade; (12.41)–(12.43) need `dim=3` (ratio of gradient moments 2 in 3-D, 3 in 2-D).
- **(i) Classifier boundaries live on the ratio.** `dispersion_regime` compares t/Λ_t ≤ 0.3 (inclusive); the JS mirror
  must compare the same ratio, not `t <= 0.3*Λ` (the two can differ in the last bit; an E10 parity row sits exactly on t = 0.3 Λ_t). `surface_layer_regime` returns "forced
  convection" for either sign of L_M — **it says nothing about stability**; take the word from
  `surface_layer_state(...)["verdict"]`.
- **(j) Outside its range a formula returns NaN, not numbers.** The log-linear wind with φ_m = 1 + βζ ran on to U < 0 in
  unstable air; it now ends at ζ = −1/β (z = |L_M|/5), the mask of `dimensionless_shear`.
- **(k) A cached table rounds its own abscissa.** `explainer_tables.json` stores y⁺ to 6 digits: parity rows that read a
  table value need rtol 1e-4 or call the function at a round y⁺.
- **(l) Labels.** A test that compares a constant of the code with its cited value is **"constant consistency"**, not V5
  (two tests renamed `_const_`; C08 and C13 recounted to 3 and 2 levels without them). **A benchmark whose source nobody
  read first-hand is not a benchmark** (Businger–Dyer → V1 "ψ_m is the integral of the coded φ_m"). Models against DNS are
  "approximate" with the band stated.
- **(m) An identity that holds for any input cannot test which input was used**: mutant R16 (`check_averaging_rules`
  ignoring `x`) survived until the test checked that the round-off of the x-rule scales like |u|/Δx.
- **(n) Guard solver messages**: `WT.log_law_crossing` now explains that no crossing exists for B below
  1/κ − ln(1/κ)/κ = 0.264 instead of surfacing brentq's text.
- **(o) Equation labels in docstrings are claims too**: `dispersion_rate_from_particles` cited "(12.115) dX/dt = u" where
  the page's (12.115) is $\frac{d}{dt}\overline{X_\alpha^2}=2\,\overline{X_\alpha\,dX_\alpha/dt}$.

## 5. Conventions used (see `knowledge/notation.md`)
- **Over-bar = ensemble average** unless a name says `time_` or `volume_`; tilde = total field; capital or over-bar =
  mean; **lower-case u, v, w and T′ are fluctuations** (so u is not "the velocity" in this chapter).
- **Lapse rate (project rule, kept):** compute with Kundu's Γ ≡ dT/dz (Γ_a = −g/C_p ≈ −9.8 K/km; stable when
  dT/dz > Γ_a) and always show the meteorological Γ_met ≡ −dT/dz (Γ_d ≈ +9.8 K/km; stable when Γ_met < Γ_d) alongside.
  T̄ and T′ in every buoyancy term are **potential** temperature; with a thermometer gradient
  N² = gα(dT/dz − Γ_a) = gα(Γ_d − Γ_met). **`gradient_richardson_thermal` requires `Gamma_a`.** The library string
  "Γ < Γa" (ch01 wording) is explained as Γ_met < Γ_d wherever it is printed.
- **Heat flux positive upward**: `wT` [K m/s], `H` = ρC_p·wT [W/m²]; upward flux ⇒ Rf < 0, L_M < 0 (unstable); L_M > 0
  stable, `inf` neutral.
- **Reynolds stress sign**: the stress is −ρ₀$\overline{u_iu_j}$; `uv_plus` = `minus_uv_plus` = −$\overline{uv}$/u_*² ≥ 0.
- **Spectra two-sided** in angular frequency [rad/s] or wavenumber [rad/m], 1/2π in the forward transform, ∫S = variance;
  returned on ω ≥ 0 only; `one_sided=True` doubles. E(K) on K ≥ 0 with ∫E dK = ē. $\overline{u^2}$ in §12.6 is **one**
  component (ē = (3/2)$\overline{u^2}$). The book's skewness and kurtosis are un-normalised (`statistics(normalized=True)`
  for the usual ones).
- **Wall variables**: y from the wall; **h = full channel height** in $dP/dx=-2\tau_0/h$ (12.90), δ = h/2; κ = von Kármán
  (`kappa`), thermal diffusivity `kappa_th`, eddy diffusivity `kappa_T`; κ = 0.41 in §12.9–12.10, **0.4 in the
  surface-layer block** (2.5 % in every wind speed, stated).
- **Jet variable** ξ = y/x (δ = x by convention, virtual origin dropped); half-widths in ξ.
- **Assumptions that switch silently**: Boussinesq with buoyancy in §12.5–12.7 → constant density in §12.8–12.10 →
  buoyancy back in §12.11; theory in ensemble averages, measurements in time averages (ergodicity, P282).
- **Default g = G0 = 9.80665**; σ² = 2νt per coordinate (12.126) (ch03's core radius used 4νt).
- **Empirical constants are never silent defaults**, and **illustrative constants are visibly not the book's** (ξ½ = 0.10,
  C_U = 7, C_Y = 4, …).
- Slips "slip #1…#18" in the notebook and `ch12.book_slips()`; colour code: mean purple · fluctuation/TKE teal · Reynolds
  stress/production orange · viscous/dissipation rose · buoyancy/temperature blue · scalar amber · ghosts muted.
