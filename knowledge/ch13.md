## 1. What clicked — the teaching spine (our words, with equation numbers)
**One set of equations with terms switched off.** Stand on the tangent plane of a thin rotating shell: only the local
vertical part of the rotation, $f=2\Omega\sin\theta$ (13.8), turns horizontal motion. Every balance of the chapter keeps two
or three terms of that set, every wave is what happens when a balance is disturbed, the Rossby radius c/|f| is where
rotation matters as much as gravity, and (ζ + f)/h is what a column remembers.
1. **C01 Thin-shell equations.** $2\boldsymbol\Omega\times\mathbf u\cong(-fv,\ fu,\ -2\Omega u\cos\theta)$ (13.7);
   $\frac{Du}{Dt}-fv=-\frac1{\rho_0}\frac{\partial p}{\partial x}+F_x$, $\frac{Dv}{Dt}+fu=-\frac1{\rho_0}\frac{\partial p}{\partial y}+F_y$
   (13.9); β-plane $f=f_0+\beta y$ (13.10) (D01, D02). §13.2 is a recap in **both lapse-rate conventions**.
2. **C02 Geostrophic balance.** $-fv=-\frac1{\rho_0}\frac{\partial p}{\partial x}$, $fu=-\frac1{\rho_0}\frac{\partial p}{\partial y}$
   (13.11)–(13.12) when $\mathrm{Ro}=\frac{U}{fL}$ (13.13) is small (D03): flow along isobars, low on the left for f > 0.
   A frictionless parcel released from rest never "settles": it traces cycloid arches about the balanced drift.
3. **C03 Thermal wind.** $\frac{\partial v}{\partial z}=-\frac g{\rho_0f}\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial x}$,
   $\frac{\partial u}{\partial z}=\frac g{\rho_0f}\frac{\partial\rho}{\partial y}$ (13.15) (D04); no gradient ⇒
   $\partial\mathbf u/\partial z=0$ (13.21), Taylor–Proudman (D05, slip #3). Two sign changes (f and the gradient) leave the
   same westerly shear in both hemispheres.
4. **C04 Surface Ekman spiral.** $\frac{d^2V}{dz^2}=\frac{if}{\nu_v}V$, V ≡ u + iv (13.27); $\delta=\sqrt{2\nu_v/f}$ (13.29)
   (D06 ★★★, slip #4): the current decays and turns at the same rate; 45° from the wind at the surface.
5. **C05 Ekman transport.** $\int_{-\infty}^0u\,dz=0$, $\int_{-\infty}^0v\,dz=-\frac{\tau}{\rho f}$ (13.30) (D07, two routes):
   the viscosity sets the depth, never the transport. Pumping $w_E=\frac1\rho\,\mathbf e_z\cdot\nabla\times(\boldsymbol\tau/f)$
   (D09, ours).
6. **C06 Bottom Ekman layer.** $u=U[1-e^{-z/\delta}\cos(z/\delta)]$, $v=Ue^{-z/\delta}\sin(z/\delta)$ (13.41) (D08): three
   forces close a triangle, the wind leans toward low pressure, air converges into lows. Largest u is 1.0670 U at 3πδ/4.
7. **C07 Shallow water.** $\frac{\partial\eta}{\partial t}+H\big(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\big)=0$,
   $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}-fv=-g\frac{\partial\eta}{\partial x}$, $\frac{\partial v}{\partial t}+fu=-g\frac{\partial\eta}{\partial y}$
   (13.45) (D10): three equations hold gravity waves, rotation and every vertical mode.
8. **C08 Vertical modes.** $\frac d{dz}\big(\frac1{N^2}\frac{d\psi_n}{dz}\big)+\frac1{c_n^2}\psi_n=0$ (13.56),
   $c_n^2\equiv gH_e$ (13.62), $c_n=\frac{NH}{n\pi}$ (13.71) (D11 ★★★): one fast barotropic mode and a ladder of slow ones.
   **Orthogonal with weight 1 for a rigid lid and for a free surface.**
9. **C09 The cubic.** $\omega^3-c^2\omega K^2-f_0^2\omega-c^2\beta k=0$ (13.76) from (13.75) (D12 ★★★, D13): two fast roots
   and one slow root that exists only because of β; which two terms balance tells you which approximation is safe.
10. **C10 Poincaré waves.** $\omega^2=f^2+gHK^2$ (13.82) (D14): no frequency below f; $c_pc_g=c^2$; long waves become
    inertial circles.
11. **C11 Kelvin wave.** $\eta=\eta_0e^{-fy/c}\cos k(x-ct)$ (13.87) (D15): v = 0 at the wall, geostrophic across the shore,
    speed $\sqrt{gH}$, coast on the right for f > 0.
12. **C12 Rossby radius and geostrophic adjustment.** Λ ≡ c/f; a released step ends as
    $\eta=\eta_0\,\mathrm{sgn}(x)(1-e^{-|x|/\Lambda})$ with a jet along the step: one third of the released potential
    energy stays as the jet's kinetic energy, two thirds leave as waves (D16 ★★★, ours).
13. **C13 Potential vorticity.** $\frac{D}{Dt}\big(\frac{\zeta+f}{h}\big)=0$ (13.94) (D17 ★★★); flow over a step (D18, slip #14).
14. **C14 Inertia–gravity waves.** $\frac{\partial^2}{\partial t^2}\nabla^2w+N^2\nabla_H^2w+f^2\frac{\partial^2w}{\partial z^2}=0$
    (13.96), $\omega^2-f^2=\frac{k^2}{m^2}(N^2-\omega^2)$ (13.112) (D19 ★★★, D20, D21): f < ω < N, **c**·**c**_g = 0.
15. **C15 Rossby waves.** $\omega=-\frac{\beta k}{k^2+l^2+f_0^2/c^2}$ (13.118) from (13.117) (D22 ★★★, D23): crests always
    west, energy west for waves longer than Λ and east for shorter; Rayleigh–Kuo: $\beta-\frac{d^2U}{dy^2}$ (13.124) must
    change sign (D24).
16. **C16 Eady problem.** $c=\frac{U_0}2\pm\frac{U_0}{\alpha H}\sqrt{\big(\frac{\alpha H}2-\tanh\frac{\alpha H}2\big)\big(\frac{\alpha H}2-\coth\frac{\alpha H}2\big)}$
    (13.141) (D25, D26 ★★★, D27): growth for αH < 2.3994, fastest at 1.6061 with σ_max = 0.30982 f (dU/dz)/N (ours).
17. **C17 Two-dimensional turbulence.** $\frac{S_1}{S_2}=\frac{K_2-K_0}{K_0-K_1}\frac{K_2+K_0}{K_1+K_0}$ (13.145) (D28): two
    invariants send energy to large scales and enstrophy to small ones; −3 range and Rhines length by dimensions (D29).

## 4. Mathematics → Python lessons (new pitfalls only; each a rule, with the measured evidence)
- **(a) A de-aliasing mask must be applied to the state, not only to the product.** `barotropic_run` masked the advective
  product with the 2/3 rule but never the initial field: white-noise input drifted +18 % in energy and +130 % in enstrophy
  over t = 4 at 64². The fix projects the start onto the retained modes (drift −6.2e-9 / −1.8e-7). The mask uses a
  **strict <**: a product landing exactly on 2/3 of Nyquist is removed (the `<=` mutant is killed).
- **(b) A parameter folded into a profile derivative can corrupt a solver's path.** Passing β as "U″ − β" to the complex-
  path Rayleigh solver moved the contour; β is its own keyword now (`rayleigh_eigs_contour(…, beta=)`), default path
  unchanged to the last bit (log; verification "rest of the suite").
- **(c) A cubic's small root is computed from its own balance, not from a general root-finder.** The slow root of
  $\omega^3-c^2\omega K^2-f_0^2\omega-c^2\beta k=0$ (13.76) is about a thousandth of the fast ones; `numpy.roots` puts its
  round-off there. Ours uses the trigonometric form and satisfies the equation to 1.9e-16; the product of roots equals
  c²βk (this identity caught a sign error in the slow root — log).
- **(d) "All roots real" is a scaling statement.** For very long waves, where the discriminant
  4(c²K² + f₀²)³ − 27(c²βk)² is negative, the cubic has one real root: the function returns NaN there and
  `shallow_water_discriminant` flags it; the explainer shades the band; examples at planetary scale stay at 35°.
- **(e) `numpy.allclose` has a default absolute tolerance of 1e-8 — larger than β.** One mutant survived for that reason;
  every comparison in the test file now goes through a helper with no absolute tolerance unless one is written.
- **(f) The boundary term of an eigenproblem belongs to the relation it appears in.** With a free surface the modes are
  orthogonal with weight 1 (the surface condition does not contain the eigenvalue); the surface term appears only in the
  energy relation (`kind="energy"`). Adding it to the weight-1 product gives an overlap of 3e-5 instead of 1e-12.
- **(g) Extremum locations are computed, never recalled.** The bottom-layer maximum is 1.0670 U at 3πδ/4, not
  $1+e^{-\pi}$ at πδ (that is where v first vanishes; u = 1.0432 U there).
- **(h) An asymptotic error order is measured before it is written.** WKB error × (Hm) → 0.230 (order 1, not 2);
  `barotropic_run` invariant drift is order 5 in dt, not 4.
- **(i) Non-dissipative schemes ring after a discontinuity.** `linear_1d_run` from a sharp step carries grid-scale ripples
  of up to about half the step behind the fronts; compare the **mean over an inertial period** with the end state (0.5 %
  over the sixth period), or smooth the start over a few cells.
- **(j) Functions with f in a denominator raise at f = 0**; every function takes either sign of f, uses abs(f) for scales
  and sign(f) for directions; `*_printed` forms refuse f ≤ 0. Mutants "sign(f) dropped" were among the most frequent.
- **(k) Sibling argument order** (Open item 8): call the Eady and Poincaré helpers by keyword.
- **(l) Box-truncated eigenproblems fail silently on radiating modes** (Open item 3): check the far-field decay rate
  Re κ against 1/y_max before trusting φ = 0 at the box edge.
- **(m) Cache sizes limit claims.** 64² viscous turbulence is "qualitative"; float16 caches reproduce to 4.1e-4 of range.
