## 1. What clicked — the teaching spine (our words, with equation numbers)
One scaling and three tools. **Viscosity only matters in a layer of thickness δ ~ L Re^{−1/2}, where advection and
diffusion balance; inside it the pressure is imposed by the outer flow.** Everything else is a solution of the resulting
parabolic equations: by similarity (no imposed length ⇒ one ODE), by an integral balance plus a one-parameter closure
(Kármán–Thwaites), or by marching. Where the outer flow decelerates too much the wall shear reaches zero, the layer
separates, and the drag becomes the pressure the wake fails to recover. Jets and the teacup reuse the same equations
without (or with a rotating) outer flow.
1. **C01 Boundary-layer scaling and equations.** u∂u/∂x ~ U²/L (9.2) = ν∂²u/∂y² ~ νU/δ̄² (9.3) ⇒ δ̄/L ~ Re^{−1/2} (9.4)
   (the ch08 √(νt) with t = L/U); continuity gives v ~ U Re^{−1/2}; the stretched variables y* = y Re^{1/2}/L, v* = v Re^{1/2}/U
   (9.6) (ε = Re^{−1/2} of (8.14)) turn steady 2-D Navier–Stokes into (9.7) (1/Re only on ∂²u*/∂x*²; **the page drops the
   squares**, R1) and (9.8) (1/Re)(u*v*_x* + v*v*_y*) = −∂p*/∂y* + (1/Re²)v*_x*x* + (1/Re)v*_y*y* (**the sign of ∂p*/∂y* was
   coded negated and caught only by review**, now pinned on a manufactured field); Re → ∞ leaves
   u∂u/∂x + v∂u/∂y = −(1/ρ)dp/dx + ν∂²u/∂y² (9.9), ∂p/∂y = 0 (9.10) (D01 ★★, 14 steps). Bernoulli at the edge gives
   −(1/ρ)dp/dx = U_e dU_e/dx (9.11) (D02). The problem is **parabolic**: x plays time, information flows downstream
   (N14; our von Mises marching solver shows it). The crude C_f ~ 2/√Re is a factor 3 above Blasius (N16).
2. **C02 Three thicknesses.** δ₉₉ (where u = 0.99U_e), δ* = ∫(1 − u/U_e)dy (9.16) — the lift of the outer streamlines, from
   equal mass fluxes of the real and a slip layer (D03) — and θ = ∫(u/U_e)(1 − u/U_e)dy (9.17) — the momentum the wall has
   removed: a control volume whose roof is a **streamline** gives ρU²θ = ∫₀ˣτ₀dx on a flat plate (D04 ★★). Same edge, different
   profile ⇒ δ* and θ change by different ratios; H = δ*/θ (Blasius 2.591).
3. **C03 Blasius reduction.** A flat plate has no length of its own, so ψ = Uδ(x)f(η), η = y/δ (9.19); the chain rule with
   η depending on x and y (the step the book skips), the cancellation of the ηf′f″ terms and "two x-only brackets must be
   proportional" give δ = √(νx/U) (9.26) and **f‴ + ½ff″ = 0 (9.27)** with f = f′ = 0 at the wall (9.28), f′ → 1 (9.29)
   (D05 ★★, 13 steps). The same move reduces Falkner–Skan and both jets.
4. **C04 Blasius numbers.** The ODE's scaling symmetry f → λf(λη) (Töpfer) turns the BVP into one IVP: solve with f″(0) = 1,
   rescale ⇒ **f″(0) = 0.332057** (V5 to 6e-15). Then η₉₉ = **4.910** (the page's 4.93 is a figure reading, R2),
   δ*/δ = 1.7208, θ/δ = 0.6641 = **2f″(0)** (by integrating (9.27) and one integration by parts, D06 steps 6–10), H = 2.591,
   τ₀ = 0.332ρU²/√Re_x (9.31), C_f = 0.664/√Re_x (9.32), C_D = 1.328/√Re_L (9.33) — **one side of the plate, as the book says**
   (R5 is a trap, not a slip; `sides=`), v∞ = 0.8604U/√Re_x (D06 ★★, 14 steps). Temporal vs spatial layer (N41):
   C_f√Re_x = 0.664 (Blasius) < 1.128 (ch08 temporal, Ut ↔ x) < 1.328 (plate mean).
5. **C05 Falkner–Skan family.** U_e = axⁿ keeps similarity with ψ = √(νxU_e)f(η) (9.34), −dp/dx = na²x^{2n−1} (9.35) and
   **f‴ + ((n+1)/2)ff″ − nf′² + n = 0 (9.36)** (D07 ★★): Blasius n = 0, Hiemenz n = 1 (f″(0) = 1.232588), n > 0 favourable,
   n < 0 adverse. The attached branch has a **fold** (saddle-node) at **n = −0.090429** (β = −0.198838) where f″(0) = 0;
   a second (reversed-flow) branch exists only for −0.0904 < n < 0 and joins the attached one at the fold — **no attached
   bounded solution below it** (R11; the book's "solutions with reverse flow below −0.0904" is not supported).
6. **C06 Kármán momentum integral.** Add u × continuity to (9.37) (conservative form (9.38)), integrate across the layer
   using ∫∂u/∂x dy = −v∞ (9.39) on [0, h] and let h → ∞ (the pieces diverge separately), Leibniz, product rule ⇒
   **τ₀/ρ = d(U_e²θ)/dx + δ*U_e dU_e/dx (9.43)** (D08 ★★, 11 steps) — exact for any layer, laminar or turbulent, but one
   equation for three unknowns (closure; Pohlhausen profiles within 6 % of Blasius θ, N55).
7. **C07 Thwaites' method.** With λ = (θ²/ν)dU_e/dx (9.44), τ₀ = μ(U_e/θ)l(λ) (9.45), H(λ) (9.46), multiply (9.43) by
   ρθ/(μU_e) (9.47) to get U_e d(θ²/ν)/dx = L(λ) = 2l − 2(2 + H)λ (9.48); the linear fit L ≈ 0.45 − 6λ (9.49) makes it a
   first-order linear ODE with integrating factor U_e⁶ ⇒ **θ²U_e⁶/ν = 0.45∫₀ˣU_e⁵dx′ + θ₀²U₀⁶/ν (9.50)** (D09 ★★). Our public
   closure is **exact on the Falkner–Skan family** (λ = nI_θ², l = I_θ f″(0), H = I_δ/I_θ, D10; Table 9.1 stays private):
   its l vanishes at **λ = −0.068148** (computed from the fold, not typed), the book's table at −0.090. Example 9.1: θ =
   0.6708√(νx/U) (+1.0 %), C_f√Re_x = 0.6575 (−1.0 %). Example 9.2 (diffuser U_e = U₁/(1 + x/L)): λ = −(0.45/4)[(1 + x/L)⁴ − 1],
   separation at **x/L = 0.158 with the book's criterion λ = −0.090**, then 0.126 with the exact FS fold −0.068148 (book first,
   both shown everywhere). Cylinder (D11, ours): U_e = 2U sin φ, λ(0) = 0.075, separation at **103.1°** (−0.09) / 100.9°
   (−0.0681) against ≈ 82° observed — ideal-flow pressure is the wrong input once the layer thickens. Measured accuracy: θ
   from −7.6 % (n = 4) to +4.4 % (n = −0.09); τ₀ ≤ 2.4 % for n ≥ 0, but −56 % at n = −0.085 (near the fold).
8. **C08 Separation.** (9.9) at the wall (u = v = 0) gives **μ(∂²u/∂y²)_wall = dp/dx** (D12): favourable ⇒ curvature < 0,
   no inflection (9.51); adverse ⇒ curvature > 0 at the wall but < 0 near the edge ⇒ an **inflection point** (9.52) (Ch. 11:
   Rayleigh); Blasius has its inflection at the wall; f‴(0) = −n. **The book's sentence "(∂²u/∂y²)₀ < 0 for n < 0" has the
   sign backwards** (R17; code and notebook follow (9.9)). Separation = τ₀ reaching zero; after it the BL equations fail
   (Goldstein, N74) and the marching solver stops.
9. **C09 Form drag and streamlining.** Our separated-pressure model (ideal C_p = 1 − 4 sin²φ up to φ_s from the **forward**
   stagnation point, a constant base pressure C_b behind) gives **C_D,p = sin φ_s(1 − (4/3)sin²φ_s − C_b)** (D13, ours): d'Alembert
   0 for φ_s = 180°; 0.884 (82°, C_b = −1.2) → 0.578 (125°, −0.6). **At fixed C_b later separation does not lower the drag
   (0.884 at 82°, 0.867 at 90°, 1.07 at 125° for C_b = −1.2); the drag falls because the wake pressure rises** — the
   non-obvious lesson of the chapter. The shaded C_p gap is the drag only when weighted by cos φ (the real plateau sits
   above the ideal curve from 82° to ≈ 132°).
10. **C10 Cylinder regimes and the Kármán street.** Rounded regime table (creeping < 1; attached eddies 4–40; laminar street
    40–200; irregular to 3000; subcritical to 3×10⁵; critical; supercritical ≥ 6×10⁵ — all labelled qualitative);
    St = fd/U ≈ 0.2 **with the cyclic frequency** (2π·0.2 = 1.26 if Ω were angular). Point-vortex street (D14 ★★★, 16 steps,
    ours; the book only states 0.28): a row sums to (Γ/2ia)cot(π(z − z₀)/a), the street moves at (Γ/2a)tanh(πb/a),
    the alternating disturbance gives eigenvalues λ = (πΓ/2a²)(±γ ± iσ) with **γ = ½ − sech²(πb/a)**, σ = sinh(πb/a)/cosh²(πb/a):
    marginal only at **cosh(πb/a) = √2, b/a = 0.28055**; facing rows unstable (π/4) at every spacing.
11. **C11 Drag crisis and sports balls.** A turbulent layer separates later (82° → 125°) **and** leaves a higher wake
    pressure, so C_D collapses near Re ≈ 3×10⁵ (model ratio 0.65 — weaker than measured; labelled illustrative). Sphere:
    the tested Morrison correlation dips to C_D ≈ 0.09 at Re ≈ 4×10⁵ with Stokes/Oseen asymptotes (book: Re_cr ≈ 5×10⁵, code
    default 3×10⁵ — flagged). Cricket swing = a seam that trips one side; negative/positive Magnus = which side is past the
    crisis (the book prints "Re < Re_cr" twice, R10).
12. **C12 Free 2-D jet.** dp/dx = 0 and [uv], [τ] → 0 at ±∞ make **J = ρ∫u²dy constant** (9.57)–(9.58) (D15; the page's
    ∂τ/∂y lacks 1/ρ, R7); ψ = u₀δf(η) with constant J forces u₀ ∝ x^{−1/3}, δ ∝ x^{2/3} and **3f‴ + ff″ + f′² = 0** (D16 ★★★,
    the book skips the differentiation); two exact derivatives ((3f″ + ff′)′ and (3f′ + f²/2)′) give f = √6 tanh(η/√6) (9.70),
    u = u₀sech²(η/√6) (9.71), C = ∫f′²dη = 4√6/3 (9.72), **ṁ = (36Jρ²νx)^{1/3} ∝ x^{1/3}** (9.73) — mass is entrained, momentum is
    not (D17); v/u₀ → ∓√6/(3√Re_x) (9.75). Half-width at 1 %: **h₉₉ = 7.3319[Cρν²x²/J]^{1/3}**; the printed 5.6152 is the 4 %
    point √6 arccosh 5 (R6, D18).
13. **C13 Wall jet.** The wall removes ordinary momentum flux (ρ∫u²dy ∝ x^{−1/4}), but integrating (9.18) from y to ∞,
    multiplying by u and integrating by parts with a variable lower limit leaves the **flux of exterior momentum flux**
    d/dx∫₀^∞u(∫_y^∞u²dy′)dy = 0 (9.80) ⇒ x u₀² = const (D19 ★★★); ψ = [νCx^{1/2}]^{1/2}f(η) gives δ ∝ x^{3/4} and
    **4f‴ + ff″ + 2f′² = 0** (the page prints coefficient 1, R3; D20 ★★★); an integrating factor f, f^{−1/2}f′ + f^{3/2}/6 =
    f∞^{3/2}/6 (the page's separation of variables lacks f^{1/2}, R4), g = √(f/f∞) and partial fractions give the implicit
    solution (9.83), f″(0) = f∞³/72, ṁ ∝ x^{1/4} (9.84) (D21 ★★★, 15 steps). **f → λf(λη) makes C and f∞ redundant** (one physical
    constant, our finding); Ψ has dimension m⁵/s³ (R8).
14. **C14 Secondary flow (the teacup).** Across a thin layer ∂p/∂z ≈ 0, so the bottom fluid feels the core's ∂p/∂R =
    ρu_e²/R but has only ρu²/R of centrifugal force: **net inward force ρ(u_e² − u²)/R** (D22) — 1000, 750, 437.5, 0 N/m³ for
    u = 0, 0.1, 0.15, 0.2 m/s (ρ = 1000, u_e = 0.2 m/s, R = 4 cm). Inflow along the floor, up the axis, out at the top: tea
    leaves gather at the centre; river bends do the same. **This is the seed of Ekman layers and spin-up (Ch. 13).**

## 4. Mathematics → Python lessons (new pitfalls only)
- **A magnitude test cannot see a sign.** (9.8) was coded with the whole right side negated (∂p*/∂y* = +(1/Re)(…) − …);
  the V7 test asserted |δp| ∝ 1/Re and passed; the review caught it by reading the page. Fix: a **sign test on a
  manufactured field** (known u, v ⇒ exact ∂p*/∂y*) — `test_bl_pressure_V1_eq_9_8_sign_on_manufactured_field` — and the
  sign mutant planted. Rule: **every signed quantity gets one test that compares the signed value, not |·|.**
- **"Slip" is a claim about the page, checked on the page.** R5 ((9.33) for one side) was listed as a book slip; the page
  says "one side of the plate". It is a trap for the reader, not a book error — slips table and `book_slips()` relabelled.
  Conversely an unrecorded slip (R17, the sign of u_yy at the wall) was found by reading the sentence against (9.9).
- **Folds are mathematics, not solver bugs.** Falkner–Skan's attached branch turns back at m = −0.090429: `solve_bvp` in m
  fails there by design. Parametrise by f″(0) (a good coordinate) to reach and pass the fold; follow the reversed branch
  separately; raise a clear error below the fold (`march_boundary_layer` with a local m below −0.0904).
- **Shooting has an exponential sensitivity e^{η²/4}** for Blasius-type problems: use the scaling symmetry (Töpfer: one IVP)
  or `solve_bvp` with continuation; shoot only where the bracket is safe (m ≥ −0.05) and with an explicit bracket (F1:
  "no sign change" for m = 1, 4 was a bracket that did not scale with f″(0)).
- **Truncation of η = ∞ depends on the member**: Gaussian tail for Blasius (η_max 12 gives 7e-12), but near the fold the
  layer is thick (η_max 10 left 0.2 % at m = −0.09) → 16 there; the wall jet's free scale f∞ sets its own η_max (F4).
- **Degenerate starts and closed forms**: Thwaites at a stagnation point is 0/0 with the finite limit θ² = 0.45ν/(6U_e′(0));
  a named wedge with n ≥ 1.5 hit it (F2). Test the limit explicitly and keep the guard that rejects θ₀ ≠ 0 there.
- **A marching scheme's order is only visible where its error dominates**: von Mises w = u² has a √ψ wall singularity —
  ψ = ψ_max σ² makes it a polynomial (order 0.98 → 2.24 after F3); BDF2 in x, not Crank–Nicolson (oscillates when the step
  is large against the wall-layer time); measure order in Δσ at fixed fine Δx and vice versa; near the fold report a
  **bracket** (n = −0.089 attached to x = 50, n = −0.12 separated at 0.27), never a separation x.
- **Two criteria, both computed**: a constant that a function can compute is never typed (`LAMBDA_SEP_FS` was typed −0.0681;
  now computed −0.0681483 lazily from the fold); the book's value stays a separate named constant (`LAMBDA_SEP_BOOK`).
- **Defaults encode physics**: `separated_pressure_drag(cp_base=None)` defaulted to the ideal value at separation and gave
  C_D,p ≈ 2.59 at 82° — the default is now the measured-scale −1.2; `falkner_skan` default η_max per member.
- **Lattice sums converge only in pairs** (+n with −n) — sum symmetrically; exact closed forms at k = 0, π/a; truncated
  sums converge at order 2 (1.99) — assert it.
- **Invert an implicit solution with brentq** (wall jet (9.83)): 6.5e-12 against the IVP; partial fractions by `sympy.apart`.
- **A test hung instead of failing**: one planted mutant (sign of the FS forcing term) made `solve_bvp` loop past 900 s.
  Solver calls in tests need a `max_nodes`/iteration cap so a wrong variant fails cleanly.
- **Validation labels**: agreement within a few percent is "approximate", not "converged" (Pohlhausen); a secondary source
  is not V5 (turbulent 0.074); a course handout is not a benchmark; "converged" needs an asserted order.

## 5. Conventions used (see `knowledge/notation.md`)
- Dimensional SI; x along the wall, y normal; u = ∂ψ/∂y, v = −∂ψ/∂x; U_e(x) edge speed; **dp/dx > 0 adverse** (ch08's
  `dpdx` book sign); −(1/ρ)dp/dx = U_eU_e′.
- **Similarity variables**: Blasius η = y/δ, δ = √(νx/U) (the ch08 η = y/√(νt) with t = x/U — factor exactly 1; the ch08
  figures' y/(2√(νt)) differs by 2); Falkner–Skan δ = √(νx/U_e); free jet δ = (Cρν²x²/J)^{1/3} ∝ x^{2/3}, η ∈ (−∞, ∞); wall jet
  δ ∝ x^{3/4}. The book's Fig. 9.7 axis ½√(n + 1)η is a different scaling (E3 offers it).
- **Falkner–Skan exponent: book n, code `m`** (`falkner_skan(m)`; docstrings say "m is the book's n"); β = 2m/(m + 1)
  (Hartree, used by Belden et al.). Thwaites uses m = −λ in some texts — not in our code.
- **Angles on bodies from the FORWARD stagnation point** (`phi_deg`: 82°, 125°, 103.1°) — ch06 measured θ from +x
  downstream, ch08's sphere θ from the downstream axis.
- **Reynolds numbers (six)**: overall U∞L/ν (9.6); local Re_x = U_e x/ν; plate Re_L; cylinder/sphere **diameter** U∞d/ν;
  jet Re_x = xu₀/ν and Re_h99; Falkner–Skan Re_x = ax^{n+1}/ν. Primer P200.
- **Thicknesses**: δ̄ (order of magnitude), δ (similarity length, not δ₉₉), δ₉₉, δ* (star also marks scaled variables in
  (9.6)–(9.8)), θ (momentum thickness — an angle everywhere else), h₉₉ (jet).
- **λ twice**: Holstein–Bohlen/Thwaites λ = (θ²/ν)dU_e/dx (C07) and the Kármán-street eigenvalue λ (C10; the code's
  `karman_street_spectrum` docstring calls the eigenvalue σ). **γ, σ in D14** are the street's dimensionless coefficients
  γ = ½ − sech²(πb/a), σ = sinh(πb/a)/cosh²(πb/a) (γ = sheet strength in ch05, c_p/c_v in ch01, ansatz variable in ch08).
- **C four times**: Blasius proportionality, free-jet ∫f′²dη = 4√6/3 (`C_fj`), wall-jet dimensional u₀ = Cx^{−1/2}, integration
  constants; L capital = body length and Thwaites' L(λ); l(λ) lower-case the shear correlation.
- St with the **cyclic** frequency f (St = fd/U ≈ 0.2); `shedding_frequency` returns f and ω = 2πf.
- g not used (except `ball_swing_deflection`, g = 9.81); ρ default 1.2 (air) in BL helpers, 1000 in the teacup.
- **Changes vs earlier chapters**: η (ch08 y/√(νt) → ch09 y/δ(x)); δ now a similarity length (ch08: diffusion thickness,
  Stokes depth); θ momentum thickness (angle elsewhere); λ Thwaites parameter and eigenvalue (ch07 wavelength; ch04 second
  viscosity coefficient; ch01/ch08 trial exponent e^{λt}, R^λ); φ angle from the forward stagnation point (ch06 θ from downstream); n Falkner–Skan exponent (ch08
  similarity exponent); ε = Re^{−1/2} plays ch08's ε = h/L; J momentum flux per span [N/m]; Ψ wall-jet invariant [m⁵/s³].
