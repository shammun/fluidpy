## 1. What clicked — the teaching spine (our words, with equation numbers)
**Three conservation laws and the gas law, written between two stations in terms of one number, M — and the factor
1 − M² that appears in every answer changes sign at the speed of sound.** C01–C02 sound and "no news upstream", C03–C04
the laws and the ruler, C05–C06 area, C07–C08 the jump, C09–C10 nozzles, C11–C12 friction and heat, C13–C16 the same in
two dimensions, where "upstream" becomes "outside the Mach cone".
1. **C01 Sound is the linear limit.** $p'\cong c^2\rho'$ (15.5), $c^2=\gamma p_0/\rho_0$ (15.6) and the linearised laws
   (15.8)–(15.11) give $\frac1{c^2}\big(\frac{\partial}{\partial t}+U_j\frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\big)^2p'-\frac{\partial^2p'}{\partial x_j\partial x_j}=0$
   (15.12) (D01, D02). Subscript 0 is the **undisturbed** state here (trap T1). The dropped term is measurable:
   ½γ(γ − 1)p₀/ρ₀² ρ′², order 2.00.
2. **C02 Two shapes sliding apart.** $p'=f(x-ct)+g(x+ct)$ (15.15) (D03); $u_1'=\frac1{\rho_0c}(f-g)$ (15.16) (D04): the air
   moves with a compression, against a rarefaction; in a stream $p'=f(x-(c+U)t)+g(x+(c-U)t)$ (15.17) (D05), and above
   M = 1 both shapes go downstream. Steepening (ours): wavelets travel at $c_0+\frac{\gamma+1}2u$, breaking time
   $2/((\gamma+1)\max(-\partial u_0/\partial x))$; γ = 2 is ch07's shallow-water wave.
3. **C03 The duct laws.** $\rho uA=\dot m$ (15.18), $\frac{d\rho}{\rho}+\frac{du}{u}+\frac{dA}{A}=0$ (15.19),
   $h_2+\frac12u_2^2-h_1-\frac12u_1^2=Q$ (15.22) (D06; holds through friction and shocks), momentum with the wall force
   (15.24)–(15.26) (D07). Everything after is these three lines with terms switched on or off.
4. **C04 The stagnation ruler.** $\frac{T_0}T=1+\frac{\gamma-1}2M^2$ (15.28), $\frac{p_0}p=[\,\cdot\,]^{\gamma/(\gamma-1)}$
   (15.29), $\frac{\rho_0}\rho=[\,\cdot\,]^{1/(\gamma-1)}$ (15.30) (D08): T₀ needs only "adiabatic", p₀ needs "isentropic";
   **T₀ is kept, p₀ is lost** when entropy is made (D09). The incompressible p + ½ρU² is 5.0 % low at M = 0.8, 0.13 % at 0.3.
5. **C05 One area, two Mach numbers.** $\frac A{A^*}=\frac1M\big[\frac2{\gamma+1}(1+\frac{\gamma-1}2M^2)\big]^{\frac12\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}$
   (15.31) (D10, "not proved in the text"); minimum 1 at M = 1; sonic ratios T*/T₀ = 0.8333, p*/p₀ = 0.5283, ρ*/ρ₀ =
   0.6339, choked $\dot m=p_0A^*\sqrt{\gamma/RT_0}\,(2/(\gamma+1))^{(\gamma+1)/(2(\gamma-1))}$ (D11, ours).
6. **C06 A supersonic nozzle widens.** $u\,du=-dp/\rho=-c^2d\rho/\rho$ (15.32, **minus restored, slip #4**),
   $d\rho/\rho=-M^2du/u$ (15.33), $\frac{du}u=-\frac1{1-M^2}\frac{dA}A$ (15.34) (D12): the only way through M = 1 is a throat
   (and a throat need not be sonic).
7. **C07 The normal shock.** $\frac{p_2}{p_1}=1+\frac{2\gamma}{\gamma+1}(M_1^2-1)$ (15.39) by dividing out the "nothing
   happens" root (D13 ★★★); $M_2^2=\frac{(\gamma-1)M_1^2+2}{2\gamma M_1^2+1-\gamma}$ (15.40, its own inverse) (D14); (15.41),
   (15.42) (D15). At M₁ = 2: M₂ = 0.5774, p 4.5, ρ 2.667, T 1.6875. Moving shock = one Galilean shift; stagnation
   quantities depend on the frame (T7).
8. **C08 Why a shock only compresses.** $\frac{S_2-S_1}{C_v}=\ln\big[\frac{p_2}{p_1}(\frac{\rho_1}{\rho_2})^\gamma\big]$ (15.43)
   (D16) is negative for M₁ < 1; weak shocks make entropy at **third** order,
   $\frac{2\gamma(\gamma-1)}{3(\gamma+1)^2}(M_1^2-1)^3$ (15.44a), $\frac{\gamma^2-1}{12\gamma^2}(\frac{\Delta p}{p_1})^3$ (15.44b)
   (D17 ★★★: first and second orders cancel identically); $p_{02}/p_{01}=e^{-\Delta S/R}=A_1^*/A_2^*$ (D18, ours) — 0.7209
   at M₁ = 2; a 10 % loss at M₁ = 1.587, half lost at 2.498. Becker's profile shows where the entropy is made.
9. **C09 Choking (ours).** Below $p_B/p_0=(2/(\gamma+1))^{\gamma/(\gamma-1)}$ the exit stays sonic and the mass flow stays at
   its plateau, met with zero slope (D19); **choked is a ceiling that the supply can still raise** ($\dot m\propto p_0/\sqrt{T_0}$, T10).
10. **C10 The Laval nozzle (ours).** Three dividing back pressures p_b > p_d > p_f (A_e/A_t = 2: 0.9372, 0.5134, 0.0939);
    between p_b and p_d a normal shock stands where $p_B/p_0=\frac{p_{02}}{p_{01}}(M_1)\,\frac p{p_0}\big|_{M_{\rm sub}}$ with
    the **new** A* (T8) (D20; nested root finding). p_B/p₀ = 0.75 puts it at M₁ = 1.763, A/A_t = 1.400.
11. **C11 Friction and heat in one formula.** (15.45) in Mach form collapses to one number A and
    $M_2^2=\frac{-(1-2A\gamma)\pm[1-2A(\gamma+1)]^{1/2}}{(\gamma-1)-2A\gamma^2}$ (15.46) (D21 ★★★); f = q = 0 gives "no change"
    and the normal shock; the root that continues M₂ = M₁ flips at M₁ = 1 (T12).
12. **C12 Everything drifts to Mach 1.** $-\frac{dp}{p_1}=\frac{1+(\gamma-1)M^2}{1-M^2}df$, $\frac{du}u=\frac{p_1}p\frac{df}{1-M^2}$
    (15.47, **numerator sign corrected, slip #11**) (D22); $\frac{dT}{T_1}=\frac{1-\gamma M^2}{1-M^2}dq$,
    $\frac{u\,du}{h_1}=\frac{(\gamma-1)M^2}{1-M^2}dq$ (15.48, **second sign corrected, slip #12**) (D23); choking where the
    discriminant vanishes, f_max, q_max (D24, ours; M₁ = 0.5: 0.2275, 0.46875). Heating cools only for 1/γ < M² < 1 (T18).
    The book's lumped f = F/(p₁A), q = Q/h₁ are primary; Fanno/Rayleigh names and 4f_FL/D are labelled "ours".
13. **C13 The Mach cone.** $\sin\mu=1/M$ (15.49) from the envelope of old fronts (D25); outside the cone is the zone of
    silence; the boom arrives after the source has passed.
14. **C14 The oblique shock.** A normal shock on the normal component, the tangential speed unchanged (D26) →
    $\tan\delta=2\cot\sigma\frac{M_1^2\sin^2\sigma-1}{M_1^2(\gamma+\cos2\sigma)+2}$ (15.53) (D27): two roots below δ_max (22.97° at
    M₁ = 2), none above (detached); the weak-wave rule $\frac{p_2-p_1}{p_1}=\frac{\gamma M_1^2}{\sqrt{M_1^2-1}}\delta$ (15.55) (D28)
    misses by 2.2 / 10.5 / 20.1 % at 1 / 5 / 10°. σ, δ here; β, θ in most sources (T13).
15. **C15 Prandtl–Meyer.** Each Mach wave turns the stream $d\delta=-\frac{\sqrt{M^2-1}}{1+\frac{\gamma-1}2M^2}\frac{dM}M$ (D29);
    summed, $\nu(M)=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}\tan^{-1}\sqrt{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma+1}(M^2-1)}-\tan^{-1}\sqrt{M^2-1}$ (15.56)
    (D30 ★★★); ν(2) = 26.38°, ν_max = 130.45°; an isentropic turn is a subtraction. Code counts an expansion turn
    positive; the book's δ = −turn (T14).
16. **C16 The supersonic flat plate.** $\frac{p-p_\infty}{p_\infty}=\frac{\gamma M_\infty^2\delta}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2-1}}$ (15.57) on each
    surface (D31) → (15.58), $C_L\cong\frac{4\alpha}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2-1}}$, $C_D\cong\frac{4\alpha^2}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2-1}}$ (15.59): no Kutta
    condition, a wave drag D = Lα without viscosity; against the exact shock–expansion result the lift is −1.1 % off at
    10° while each surface is 20–25 % off (the errors cancel at second order). Slope 4/√3 = 2.309 at M = 2 vs ch14's 2π.

## 4. Mathematics → Python lessons (new pitfalls only; each a rule, with the measured evidence)
- **(a) One gas per call.** A function that takes both `gamma` and `gas` must take γ from `gas` and refuse a
  disagreeing `gamma`: the old `mass_flow_vs_back_pressure` used γ = 1.4 with a rocket gas's R and returned 20.966
  instead of 20.149 kg/s (4.1 %). No test reached it because the only dimensional test used air — **test every
  gas-carrying function with γ ≠ 1.4**.
- **(b) 0·∞ poisons a sum at a singular point.** Multiplying each increment by 1/(1 − M²) separately gave NaN for every
  key at M = 1 whenever one increment was zero; **form the driver first, then divide** (F1). The sign of ±∞ is the
  subsonic side's; "exactly zero" means exactly in doubles (0.1 − 0.04 − 0.06 is −6.9e-18).
- **(c) A bare formula outside its range returns plausible nonsense.** `oblique_shock(sigma < μ₁)` returned an
  "expansion shock" with p₂/p₁ = 0.517 and ΔS < 0 (F2); `deflection_angle` is odd in σ, so its sign is **not** a validity
  test. Guard in the callers: raise for scalars, NaN + `attached=False` for arrays, with a round-off allowance (asin(1/M)
  gives M sin σ one ulp below 1 for > 20 of 800 Mach numbers).
- **(d) Cancellation at a branch point**: write M₁² − 1 as (M₁ − 1)(M₁ + 1) and switch to the series below |m| = 0.02
  (relative error ≤ 3e-15 down to |M₁ − 1| = 1e-12; the naive form lost 6 digits). Clamp ν + turn ∈ (−1e-12, 0) to the
  sonic state (683 of 1600 return trips were NaN before).
- **(e) Printed slips coded as options a test must fail** (third time: ch13, ch14) — here they **change the physics**:
  `printed_15_47` misses dp/p by exactly 2(γ − 1)M²/(1 + (γ − 1)M²) (7 % at M = 0.3, sign wrong at 2); `printed_15_48`
  has the opposite sign of u du/h everywhere; `printed_15_32` would make a widening duct slow a supersonic stream.
  Each confirmed three ways: finite differences of the closed form, sympy on the linearised laws, sympy on the finite laws.
- **(f) A square-root branch point limits accuracy, not the code**: at f_max the outlet Mach number is ½(γ + 1)√|D|
  from 1, so 2 ulp in D give 1.3e-8 in M₂ — assert the conditioning bound, never loosen elsewhere.
- **(g) Two inverses: the physics chooses the bracket** (`branch=` keyword-only, no default, on every two-valued inverse);
  nested root finders (`laval_shock_location`) bracket the outer search inside the range where the inner one exists.
- **(h) Three air-constant sets in one namespace** (CP_BOOK 1004.5; `PerfectGas()` R = 287.058 → C_p = 1004.70;
  `AIR_BOOK` 287.0): 379.6416 K against 379.6255 K for the same flow; a 1e-6 cross-check fails unless `cp=gas.cp`.
- **(i) A re-exported function under a name that means something else in this chapter is a trap**: ch04's
  `stagnation_pressure` (p + ½ρU²) under `ch15.stagnation_pressure` → a warning wrapper plus the explicit alias
  `stagnation_pressure_incompressible`; the test pins the 5 % gap at M = 0.8.
- **(j) Sibling argument orders** again: `ackeret_pressure(delta, M_inf)` against `weak_wave_pressure(M, delta)` for the same
  (15.57) — a swap gives NaN, not silence; call by keyword.
- **(k) Measure the order of an approximation before writing it**: the plate's linear theory is second order in α
  (C_L 2.02, 2.00, 1.99), one surface first order (1.02) — the analysis and a docstring said first.
- **(l) Labels audited mechanically** (`label_audit.py`): every claimed V-level must appear in a cited test that calls the
  function; tables are "V7 (structure)" — there is no analytic truth in a table.
